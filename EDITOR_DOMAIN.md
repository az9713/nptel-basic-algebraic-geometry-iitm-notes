# Domain: classical algebraic geometry

Reader: a mathematically mature graduate who needs the subfield introduced.
Default field k is algebraically closed. Varieties are reduced and irreducible;
algebraic sets may be reducible. Projective coordinate rings retain a chosen
embedding, while local rings and function fields are intrinsic.

Check all uses of the Nullstellensatz, finite generation, irreducibility,
dimension, locality, separatedness, birationality, regularity and smoothness.
Do not identify a bijective morphism with an isomorphism. Do not infer that an
abstract local-ring isomorphism identifies marked points or extends globally.
Do not describe a singular locus as a manifold boundary. Distinguish regular
local rings, analytic manifolds over C, and smoothness over a base field.
The Jacobian conjecture is discussed as a conjecture, with characteristic-zero
hypotheses, never as an established consequence of the inverse function theorem.

Review claims for statement, defined objects, proof or explicit cited status,
hypothesis failures and concrete examples. Source coverage and mathematical
correctness are separate audit dimensions.
