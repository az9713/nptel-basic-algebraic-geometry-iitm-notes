# Notation and dependencies

- k: algebraically closed field, unless a section explicitly changes the field.
- A^n: affine n-space over k; P^n: projective n-space over k.
- V(S): common zero locus; I(T): ideal of polynomials vanishing on T.
- k[X]: affine coordinate ring k[x_1,...,x_n]/I(X).
- D(f): nonvanishing locus of a regular function f on an affine algebraic set.
- O_X(U): regular functions on U; O_{X,p}: germs of regular functions at p.
- m_p: maximal ideal of functions/germs vanishing at p.
- k(X): function field of an irreducible variety X.
- S(X): homogeneous coordinate ring for a specified projective embedding.
- S_(f): degree-zero subring of the homogeneous localization S_f.
- T_p X: Zariski tangent space, dual to m_p/m_p^2 at a k-rational point.
